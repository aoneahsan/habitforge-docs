Habits

  • Habits

    Create a habit, check in, and read what the rope says: streaks, rope strength, points and levels, and how to pause or delete a habit.

  • Create a habit

    Create a habit with a name, category, kind and schedule, plus the optional cue, routine, reward, craving and investment fields from The Power of Habit.

    • habits

  • Check-ins and streaks

    A check-in tells HabitForge you did a habit today. Consecutive check-ins build a streak and earn points, and your longest streak is kept even when a run breaks.

    • habits
    • streaks

  • Rope strength

    HabitForge's habit-strength meter. The rope thickens from a thread towards a chain as you keep going, and frays where you stopped. Calculated, not predicted.

    • habits
    • visualization

  • Levels and points

    Points come from check-ins and achievements across all your habits, and enough of them raise your level through ten forge tiers, from Spark to Forge Master.

    • habits
    • levels

  • Manage habits

    Edit a habit's details, pause it while you are away, or delete it for good. Pausing keeps your history; deleting removes the habit permanently.

Features

  • Features

    What HabitForge does beyond the daily check-in: analytics, achievements, the community, themes, reminders, AI and Speak, and the page tours.

  • Analytics

    HabitForge's analytics turn your check-in history into weekly charts, so you can see which habits are holding and where consistency is slipping.

    • analytics

  • Achievements

    Achievements are milestones HabitForge unlocks as you build habits — streaks, points, and consistency goals — each with a celebration moment and bonus points.

    • achievements

  • Community

    HabitForge's community adds a board, challenges, friends and a leaderboard, with report and block tools. A new profile is visible by default; you can change it.

    • community

  • Themes and customiser

    HabitForge ships light and dark themes plus a customiser for appearance, accent colour, radius, scaling and font size. Your choices follow your account.

    • themes
    • customization

  • Reminders and notifications

    An in-app bell tells you what happened while you were away, and your own device raises the reminders: one a day, and one per habit on the days it is due.

    • notifications
    • reminders

  • Page tours and How HabitForge works

    Every main HabitForge page has a short tour behind the signpost button in the header, and the How HabitForge works page explains the whole app in six steps.

    • tours
    • getting-started

  • AI and Speak

    HabitForge drafts a habit's loop, reads your week back, helps word a post, and turns what you say into suggested check-ins, habits and posts. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

    • ai
    • speak

Apps & offline

  • Apps & offline

    Run HabitForge as a web app that installs to your home screen and works offline, or as the Android app on Google Play.

  • Install and offline

    HabitForge is a PWA — install it to your home screen from any browser and keep checking in offline. Check-ins made without a connection sync when you reconnect.

    • pwa
    • offline

  • The Android app

    HabitForge on Android is a native app with over-the-air updates, so fixes reach you without a store release. Install it from Google Play.
