Built and maintained by the HabitForge team · LinkedIn · GitHub

Your consistency, drawn as a rope

Each habit is drawn as a rope that thickens from a thin thread towards a chain as you keep showing up, and frays where you stopped. A deterministic picture, not a prediction.

Check in, build a streak

Mark a habit done on each day it is due. Consecutive check-ins build a streak and earn points, around the cue → routine → reward loop.

Points, levels and achievements

Showing up earns points, and points earn ten levels from Spark to Forge Master. Milestones unlock achievements, and each one is worth points.

Read your week

Weekly analytics turn your check-in history into charts, so you can see which habits are holding and where consistency is slipping.

The community, and who sees you

Post to a board, add friends, and join challenges scored against a target. A new profile is public and on the leaderboard until you change one switch and one separate choice. Neither is opt-in.

Web now, Android in testing

Use it in any browser, or add the web app to your home screen. It works offline and syncs when you reconnect. The Android app is on Google Play.
