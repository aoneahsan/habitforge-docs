# Contact HabitForge

HabitForge is a free product maintained by one engineer, [Ahsan Mahmood](./author). There is no large support desk — but messages are read, and feedback genuinely shapes what gets built. This page lists the best way to reach out for each kind of question.

## The fastest channels

- **Email:** [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com) — for anything: support, feedback, privacy questions, or partnership inquiries.
- **Documentation issues:** open an issue on the public docs repository at [github.com/aoneahsan/habitforge-docs/issues](https://github.com/aoneahsan/habitforge-docs/issues) — best for errors, gaps, or clarifications in these docs.

A short, specific message gets a faster, more useful reply. Where relevant, please include your platform (web, iOS, Android, or the browser extension), what you expected, and what actually happened.

## What to send where

### Report a bug

Email [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com) with:

- The **platform** and browser/OS version.
- The **steps to reproduce** the problem.
- What you **expected** versus what **happened**.
- A screenshot if it helps.

HabitForge reports crashes through Sentry automatically, but a description from you adds the context that a stack trace cannot.

### Request a feature

Email a short note describing the outcome you want — not just the mechanism. The [Roadmap](../reference/roadmap) shows what is already planned (and what is explicitly out of scope), so it is worth a glance first; if your idea is not there, that is exactly the kind of message worth sending.

### Ask a privacy or data question

For account, data, or privacy questions, email [aoneahsan@gmail.com](mailto:aoneahsan@gmail.com). You can also handle the common cases yourself:

- **Export your data** at any time — see [Export your data](../account/export).
- **Delete your account and data** from **Profile → Delete account** — see [Delete your account](../account/deletion).
- Review what HabitForge stores in [Privacy basics](../getting-started/privacy-basics).

### Get help using the app

Many "how do I…" questions are already answered in these docs:

- [FAQ](../reference/faq) — the most common questions.
- [Getting started](../getting-started/welcome) — sign-up and your first habit.
- [Cross-device sync](../theme/sync) and [Offline-first tracking](../theme/offline) — data and connectivity.

If the answer is not there, email and it will get answered — and often added to the docs so the next person finds it.

## Response expectations (honest)

Because HabitForge is free and run by one person, replies are best-effort and may take a little time — there is no guaranteed support SLA. Clear, reproducible reports are prioritized because they are the ones that lead to fixes. Thank you for using HabitForge and for taking the time to get in touch.
