# Your Privacy Rights in HabitForge (Summary)

This page is a plain-language summary of the data-protection rights you have when using **HabitForge** under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and similar laws — and how to exercise each one. Exercising your rights is free of charge.

> **This is a summary. The authoritative, current version is the in-app rights page at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/gdpr-rights](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/gdpr-rights).** If anything here differs from that page, the in-app page governs. This documentation page is provided for convenience only.

## Your rights at a glance

- **Right to access** — obtain confirmation that your data is being processed and a copy of it. *How:* request a copy by email; provided in a commonly used format within 30 days.
- **Right to rectification** — have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed. *How:* update your information directly in the app settings, or contact the developer.
- **Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — request deletion of your personal data. *How:* use the account deletion page or contact the developer.
- **Right to restrict processing** — limit how your data is processed in certain situations. *How:* contact the developer; your data will be stored but not processed further until the restriction is lifted.
- **Right to data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and move it elsewhere. *How:* request an export; provided in JSON format within **7 days**.
- **Right to object** — object to processing for direct marketing or based on legitimate interests. *How:* opt out of marketing in settings, or contact the developer for other objections.
- **Right to withdraw consent** — withdraw consent where processing relies on it. *How:* disable analytics in settings to withdraw analytics consent; contact the developer for other cases.

## Exporting your own data

HabitForge lets you export your data yourself, free and in the browser, from the Settings page. You can download your full data as a JSON file, or download your habits and completions as separate CSV files. This satisfies the right to data portability without waiting for a manual request. See the [export your data guide](../account/export.md).

## Deleting your account and data

The right to erasure (GDPR Article 17) is honored through the account deletion process: email a deletion request, verify your identity, and your personal data is permanently deleted within **30 days maximum**. Some anonymized aggregate data and limited legally required records may be retained. See the [account deletion guide](../account/deletion.md) or the in-app [account deletion page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/account-deletion).

## Response times

- **Access requests:** within 30 days
- **Data export:** within 7 days
- **Account deletion:** within 30 days
- **Other requests:** within 30 days

If a request is especially complex, the developer will notify you within 30 days and explain any delay.

## Identity verification

To protect your data, you may be asked to verify your identity before a request is processed — typically by confirming your email address and clicking a verification link. You will never be asked for your password by email.

## Cost and complaints

Exercising your rights is **free of charge**. If a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive (particularly if repetitive), a reasonable fee may apply or the request may be refused. If you believe your rights have been violated, you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in the EU, your local Data Protection Authority. You are encouraged to contact the developer first so concerns can be resolved directly.
