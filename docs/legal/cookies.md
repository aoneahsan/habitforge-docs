# HabitForge Cookie Policy (Summary)

This page is a plain-language summary of the cookies and similar technologies (like local storage) that the **HabitForge** website uses, what each is for, and how you can control them. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help remember your preferences and improve your experience.

> **This is a summary. The authoritative, current version is the in-app cookie policy at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/cookie-policy](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/cookie-policy).** If anything here differs from that page, the in-app page governs. This documentation page is provided for convenience only.

## Cookies HabitForge uses

The web app uses three categories of cookies:

| Category | Example | Required? | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Essential** | Authentication cookies | Yes | Keep you signed in and maintain your session securely (session / 30 days). |
| **Functional** | Preference cookies | No | Remember settings like theme and language (about 1 year). |
| **Analytics** | Firebase Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Amplitude | No | Understand how the app is used so it can be improved. |

### Essential cookies

Necessary for the app to work — without them you cannot sign in or use basic features. You cannot opt out of essential cookies.

### Functional cookies

Remember your preferences for a personalized experience. The app still works without them, but some settings may not be remembered.

### Analytics cookies

Help measure how features are used so the app can improve. They collect anonymous information such as page views, features used, and errors. You can opt out of analytics in your account settings — disabling them does **not** affect app functionality.

## Third-party services that may set cookies

The analytics providers below may set their own cookies on the website:

- **Firebase Analytics (Google)** — product-usage and performance analytics (processor).
- **Amplitude** — detailed event tracking for feature-usage analysis (processor).
- **Microsoft Clarity** — session recordings and heatmaps for UX improvement (**website only**, independent controller).
- **Yandex Metrica** — traffic and behavior analytics (**website only**, independent controller).
- **Sentry** — crash and error monitoring on production builds (processor).

The two session-replay providers (Microsoft Clarity and Yandex Metrica) run on the website only and are never initialized inside the mobile app.

## Local storage

In addition to cookies, the web app uses local storage to keep:

- Your authentication token (to keep you signed in)
- App preferences and settings
- Cached data for offline functionality

Local storage is cleared when you sign out or clear your browser data.

## Managing your cookie preferences

You have several options:

- **In-app settings** — disable analytics tracking in your account settings to stop analytics cookies from being set.
- **Browser settings** — most browsers let you block or control cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect app functionality.
- **Device settings** — on mobile, you can limit ad tracking in your device settings.

> **Note:** Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from using HabitForge. Disabling functional cookies may affect some features. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect app functionality.
