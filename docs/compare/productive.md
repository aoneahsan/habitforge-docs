# Moving from Productive to HabitForge

**Productive offers templates, guided challenges and reminders that fire at a place. HabitForge has one daily reminder at a time you choose, and you write the habits yourself.**

## Should you move at all?

If you rely on habit templates or a reminder that fires when you reach the gym, stay. HabitForge has neither.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** List the habits you still do. The pages we read do not mention an export, so write them down.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add each one by hand. If you liked starting from a template, the **Draft with AI** button on a new habit fills in the loop from its name, and [you can change every word](../features/ai-and-speak.md). [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** A paused habit stays paused here too: see [manage habits](../habits/manage.md). Guided challenges against other users become [community challenges](../features/community.md), which you or a friend set up.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in Productive. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
