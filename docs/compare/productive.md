# Moving from Productive to HabitForge

**Productive gives you a library of habit templates, guided challenges and reminders that fire when you reach a place. HabitForge gives you one daily reminder at a time you choose, and you write the habits yourself.**

## Templates or your own words

If you rely on templates, or on a reminder that goes off when you arrive at the gym, stay. HabitForge has neither.

Come over if the week-long trial left you wanting a plan that stays free, a web app, and a rope that keeps what you kept where a chain counts from the last miss. [What HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do) has the rest.

## Bringing your habits

1. **Write down the habits you still do.** Productive’s pages do not mention an export.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Start each habit from its name.** If you miss the templates, press **Draft with AI** on a new habit. It fills in the loop from the name; nothing is saved until you press Save, and [you can change every word](../features/ai-and-speak.md) before you do. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Pause what was paused.** A habit you had paused in Productive can be paused here as well, history kept. See [manage habits](../habits/manage.md).
5. **Set one reminder time.** There is no location trigger, so choose the hour you are usually at the place.
6. **Recreate a guided challenge as a community one.** You or a friend sets it up in [the community](../features/community.md); nobody guides it.
7. **Read the rope, not a chain.** Each one [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) and thickens as you keep going.
