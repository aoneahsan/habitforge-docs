# Moving from HabitNow to HabitForge

**HabitNow is a to-do list, a calendar and a habit tracker in one Android app, paid for once. HabitForge is only the habit part.**

## One app for everything, or one for habits

If your to-do list and your habits belong in the same app, stay. HabitForge will not hold your tasks or your calendar.

If habits are the whole job, HabitForge gives each one a cue, routine and reward, a rope you can watch thicken, a web app beside the Android one, and other people tracking with you. It has no widgets; the rest of [what it does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do) is worth a look.

## Bringing your habits

1. **Make a backup in HabitNow.** HabitForge cannot read it, but it is your record. Then open the habit list and copy from there.
2. **Leave the tasks.** One-off to-dos and calendar entries do not belong here. Decide where they go before you start.
3. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md)** and add the habits one by one. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Drop the timers.** A check-in here is a tick that you did it, with no clock attached.
5. **[Install the Android app](../apps/android.md) and set a [daily reminder](../features/reminders-and-notifications.md).** It is the nearest thing to the widget you had.
6. **Give it two weeks before you uninstall HabitNow.** Every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md).
