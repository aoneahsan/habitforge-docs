# Moving from HabitNow to HabitForge

**HabitNow is a to-do list, a calendar and a habit tracker in one Android app. HabitForge is only the habit part, so your tasks and calendar would need a home of their own.**

## Should you move at all?

If you want your to-do list and your habits in the same app, stay. HabitForge will not hold your tasks.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** In HabitNow, make a backup and keep it. HabitForge cannot read it, but the app's own list of habits is what you are copying from. Leave the one-off tasks behind.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add each habit by hand. Timers do not carry over: a check-in here is a tick that you did it, with no clock attached. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** Install [the Android app](../apps/android.md), then set a [daily reminder](../features/reminders-and-notifications.md). HabitNow's widgets have no match here.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in HabitNow. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
