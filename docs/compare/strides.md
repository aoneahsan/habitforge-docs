# Moving from Strides to HabitForge

**Strides tracks numbers and dated targets as well as habits, with a pace that tells you whether you are on track. HabitForge tracks whether you showed up, so “12 books by June” has no place here.**

## Targets or habits

If most of what you track is a number heading for a date, stay. HabitForge has no targets of that kind and no SMART goals.

Come over if the habits were always the part that mattered: the cue, routine and reward in your own words, a rope that frays where Strides shows a success rate dropping, and a community that works on Android and the web. Read [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do) before you move.

## Bringing your habits

1. **If you are on Strides Plus, export your data.** That is where the export sits. Keep the file; HabitForge cannot read it. On the free plan, list the habits by hand.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Bring only the daily and weekly habits.** Leave the dated goals and the number trackers behind. A bad habit you tracked is **break** here. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Replace your accountability partner with a challenge.** On Strides that runs through iCloud on Apple devices. Here, a [challenge](../features/community.md) works between anyone with an account, on Android or the web.
5. **Let the rope start thin.** It [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in.
