# Moving from Strides to HabitForge

**Strides tracks numbers and dated targets as well as habits, with an on-track pace. HabitForge tracks whether you showed up, so a target of 12 books by June has no place here.**

## Should you move at all?

If most of what you track is a number heading for a date, stay. HabitForge has no targets of that kind.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** Export your data if you are on Strides Plus, where that feature sits, and keep the file. HabitForge cannot read it. Otherwise list the habits by hand.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add only the daily and weekly habits, by hand. A bad habit you tracked goes in as **break**. Leave the dated goals behind. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** Accountability partners on Strides work through iCloud on Apple devices. Here, a [challenge](../features/community.md) works on Android and the web, between anyone with an account.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in Strides. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
