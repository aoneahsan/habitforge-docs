# Moving from Do Habits to HabitForge

**Do Habits is an iPhone tracker for goals you can log several times a day, with Apple Health and a vacation mode. HabitForge counts one check-in per habit per day, on the web and Android.**

## Many logs a day, or one

If you log the same habit five times a day, or you need Apple Health and your iPhone widgets, stay. HabitForge has no iOS app, no widgets and no health sync ([the full list](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do)).

If the free three habits ran out, the free plan here holds 30. You also get a web and Android app, a rope where Do Habits has a chain, and friends and challenges around it.

## Bringing your habits

1. **Export the CSV from Do Habits.** HabitForge cannot read it; keep it for the list and for your old numbers.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Rethink every multi-log goal.** “Drink water, 8 times” becomes one habit you check in once, when the day’s water is done. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Use pause for vacation mode.** [Pausing a habit](../habits/manage.md) keeps its history; the current run starts again when you resume.
5. **Write the cue on the habit you log most.** One sentence: what comes right before it.
6. **Leave Do Habits on your phone for the first week.** Your rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) and will look thin next to a long chain.
