# Moving from Do Habits to HabitForge

**Do Habits is an iPhone tracker for goals you can log several times a day, with Apple Health and a vacation mode. HabitForge counts one check-in per habit per day, on the web and Android.**

## Should you move at all?

If you log the same habit several times a day, or you need Apple Health and your iPhone widgets, stay.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** Export your data from Do Habits to a CSV file and keep it. HabitForge cannot read it, but it gives you the list.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add each habit by hand. A habit you logged five times a day needs a rethink here: write it as the one thing you did today. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** Vacation mode has a counterpart: [pause the habit](../habits/manage.md). Your history stays, and the current run starts again when you resume.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in Do Habits. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
