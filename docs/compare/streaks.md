# Moving from Streaks to HabitForge

**Streaks lives on Apple devices. HabitForge has no iOS app: on an iPhone you would use the web app in the browser, and lose the widgets and the Apple Health link.**

## iPhone or not

If you are keeping your iPhone and Apple Watch, Streaks fits better. It is one $5.99 purchase, it has widgets everywhere, and Apple Health ticks some tasks off for you. HabitForge does none of that; [the full list of gaps](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do) is short but real.

Moving makes sense if you are going to Android, or you work at a computer most of the day. You also get a rope that frays on a missed day where Streaks sends the count back to zero, and a community rather than a shared task.

## Bringing your habits

1. **Screenshot your Streaks list.** It holds up to 24 tasks; we found no export on its pages.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md)** on the web, or on Android if that is the reason you are moving.
3. **Turn negative tasks into break habits.** Everything else is build or maintain. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **On an iPhone, [add the web app to your home screen](../apps/install-and-offline.md).** It is the closest you get to an icon.
5. **Tick the Health-driven ones by hand.** Nothing here reads Apple Health. A check-in is something you do on purpose.
6. **Expect day one to look thin.** Your rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) however long your Streaks count was.
7. **Move shared tasks into a challenge.** A task you shared through iCloud becomes a [challenge](../features/community.md) you both join, from any device with a browser.
