# Moving from Way of Life to HabitForge

**Way of Life gives each habit a colour for each day, green or red, and it has been doing that since 2010. HabitForge draws the same days as a rope and puts the cue, routine and reward beside it.**

## Green, red, or a rope

If a minute a day of marking green or red is all you want, with a diary note for what set a bad habit off and charts going back years, Way of Life already does that well.

Come over if the notes have turned into the useful part. Here the trigger lives on the habit as its **cue**, and the rope shows how strong the habit is, where a chain shows how many days. Check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do) first: no widgets, no Siri.

## Bringing your habits

1. **On Android, export your data as CSV or JSON.** Keep the file. HabitForge cannot read it, but it holds your years.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Re-add each habit.** Every one you were marking red is a **break** habit here. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Copy your best diary note into the cue.** Pick the note that names what set it off most often. That sentence is the cue.
5. **Set a [daily reminder](../features/reminders-and-notifications.md).** It stands in for the widgets, Siri and Shortcuts you had on Apple devices.
6. **Do not compare colours with ropes in week one.** Your rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md); your Way of Life chart has years behind it.

Way of Life’s pages do not say whether a habit carries its cue, routine and reward, whether a missed day keeps the days before it, or whether it has pausing, friends or challenges, a web app, offline check-ins, AI help, a household plan, health sync or open source code, so those rows are left out of the table.
