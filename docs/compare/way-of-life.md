# Moving from Way of Life to HabitForge

**Way of Life gives each day a colour for each habit. HabitForge draws the same days as a rope, and adds the cue, routine and reward beside it.**

## Should you move at all?

If a minute a day of marking green or red is all you want, with a diary note for what set a bad habit off, Way of Life already does that well.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** On Android, export your data from Way of Life as CSV or JSON and keep the file. HabitForge cannot read it, but you will want the list.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add each habit by hand. A bad habit you were marking red is a **break** habit here. The note you wrote about what set it off belongs in its **cue** field. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** Way of Life has widgets, Siri and Shortcuts on Apple devices. HabitForge has none of those, so set a [daily reminder](../features/reminders-and-notifications.md) to take their place.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in Way of Life. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
