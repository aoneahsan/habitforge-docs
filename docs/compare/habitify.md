# Moving from Habitify to HabitForge

**Habitify runs on watch, phone, Mac and web, with reminders stacked on other habits, location reminders, Apple Health and Zapier. HabitForge runs on the web and Android, with one daily reminder.**

## Every device, or two

If you live on an Apple Watch, or you depend on Zapier or location reminders, stay.

Come over if three free habits is too few (the free plan here holds 30), or you want cue, routine and reward on the habit itself, a rope that shows strength rather than a tick, and AI that drafts a habit or a weekly reflection when you ask. The gaps are listed in [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do).

## Bringing your habits

1. **List your habits, and beside each, the habit its reminder is stacked on.** The pages we read do not mention an export.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Turn each stack into a cue.** “After I brush my teeth” goes in the **cue** field. It is a sentence you read; nothing fires when you get there. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Use pause where you used Off mode.** [Manage habits](../habits/manage.md).
5. **Move friend challenges and leaderboards to the [community](../features/community.md).** Your friends need their own accounts.
6. **Pick one reminder time for the day,** since there is one.
7. **Keep Habitify for a week.** Your rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md).

Habitify’s pages also do not say whether a habit carries its cue, routine and reward, shows more than a streak number, or keeps the days before a miss, or whether it tracks habits you want to stop or has offline check-ins, AI help, a household plan, widgets or open source code, so those rows are left out of the table along with the export.
