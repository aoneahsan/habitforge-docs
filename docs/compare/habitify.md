# Moving from Habitify to HabitForge

**Habitify runs on watch, phone, Mac and web, with stacked reminders, location reminders and Apple Health. HabitForge runs on the web and Android, with one daily reminder.**

## Should you move at all?

If you live on an Apple Watch, or you use Zapier or location reminders, stay.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** List your habits. The pages we read do not mention an export, so write them down, and note which habit each stacked reminder hangs off.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add each habit by hand. A habit you stacked on another one goes in the **cue** field here: "after I brush my teeth". It is a sentence you read, and nothing fires when you get there. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** Friend challenges and leaderboards have a home in the [community](../features/community.md). Off mode for a break is [pause](../habits/manage.md) here.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in Habitify. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
