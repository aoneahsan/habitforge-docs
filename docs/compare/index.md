# Moving to HabitForge

**There is one guide per app, each with the steps for moving across and a table of what we read on that app's own pages, with the date we read it.** HabitForge has no importer, so every move is done by hand, and each guide says what will not come with you.

To decide whether to move, start with [the comparison page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/compare). These guides are for once you have decided.

## What HabitForge does not do

- **No iOS app.** On an iPhone or iPad, HabitForge [runs in the browser](../apps/install-and-offline.md).
- **No home screen widgets.** To check in, you open the app.
- **No health or wearable sync.** A check-in is something you do on purpose.
- **No guided way to replace a habit.** You can mark a habit as one to break and write down its cue and reward. Nothing here walks you through keeping those while you change the routine.
