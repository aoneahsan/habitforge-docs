# Moving from everyday to HabitForge

**everyday shows a board of chains on the web, on your phone and in every new browser tab. HabitForge shows a rope per habit, on the web and Android, with no browser extension.**

## The board in every tab

If seeing your board each time you open a tab is what keeps you honest, or you want email reminders, stay. HabitForge has neither.

If three free habits is not enough, the free plan here holds 30. You also get the cue and reward written next to each habit, a rope that frays where a chain breaks, and people in the room with you. The things we lack are in [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do).

## Bringing your habits

1. **Write down what is on your board.** The pages we read do not mention an export.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Re-add them with a cue each, if you know it.** [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Use pause where you used skip days.** A skipped day on everyday is close to a [paused habit](../habits/manage.md) here.
5. **Get ready for your first miss to look different.** A chain goes back to nothing when it breaks. The rope [frays and keeps what you kept](../habits/rope-strength.md).
6. **Keep the extension for a week** while the rope is still a thread.
