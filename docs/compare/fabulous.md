# Moving from Fabulous to HabitForge

**Fabulous coaches you through whole routines, with audio sessions and a coach you can book. HabitForge has no coach. You write your own habits, and AI drafts the wording only when you ask.**

## Coach or tracker

If being guided through your morning is the reason it works, stay. HabitForge will not walk you through anything, and nothing [guides you through replacing one habit with another](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do).

Come over if you want a tracker, not a coach: your own habits, each with its cue and reward, a rope that shows where you stand, a free plan with no end date, and a price on the page.

## Bringing your habits

1. **Notice what you still do without the audio.** Two or three parts of the routine, no more. Those are your first habits. Fabulous’s pages do not mention an export.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Make each part its own habit, with the step before it as the cue.** “After my morning coffee.” [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
4. **Ask for a draft when the words will not come.** [Draft with AI](../features/ai-and-speak.md) fills the loop from the name. It is a suggestion; nothing is kept until you press Save.
5. **Find people, not a coach.** The [community](../features/community.md) is others tracking beside you. None of them is there to guide you.
6. **Give the rope a week.** It [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md).
