# Moving from Fabulous to HabitForge

**Fabulous coaches you through routines with audio sessions and a coach you can book. HabitForge has no coach. You write your own habits, and AI can draft the wording when you ask it to.**

## Should you move at all?

If being guided through your morning is the reason it works for you, stay. HabitForge will not walk you through anything.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** Pick the two or three parts of your Fabulous routine you still do unprompted. Those are your first habits. The pages we read do not mention an export.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add each part as its own habit, with what comes before it as the **cue**: "after my morning coffee". [Draft with AI](../features/ai-and-speak.md) fills the loop from the name if the words will not come. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** The [community](../features/community.md) is people tracking alongside you. Nobody on it is a coach.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in Fabulous. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
