# Moving from HabitKit to HabitForge

**HabitKit is a grid you fill one tile a day: no account, no feed, nobody watching. HabitForge needs a Google sign-in and puts other people beside you, so it is a different kind of quiet.**

## Should you move at all?

If nobody watching is the point, stay. A new HabitForge profile is public and ranked until you change it in Settings.

Also check [what HabitForge does not do](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do). HabitForge has no iOS app, no home screen widgets and no health sync, and nothing guides you through replacing one habit with another.

## The move, step by step

HabitForge has no importer, so this is done by hand.

1. **Get your list out.** Export a backup from HabitKit and keep it. HabitForge cannot read it, so the list you copy from is your own.
2. **Sign in.** Open HabitForge and [sign in with Google](../getting-started.md). There is no password to set.
3. **Add your habits.** Add each habit by hand. A habit you are quitting is a **break** habit. Then go to Settings and [switch off the public profile and leaderboard](../features/community.md) if you want it quiet here too. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md) has every field.
4. **Write the loop on the ones that matter.** Add the cue, routine and reward to two or three habits first. The rest can stay as a name until you need more.
5. **Set up what surrounds them.** Widgets do not come across. Set a [daily reminder](../features/reminders-and-notifications.md) if the grid on your home screen was your reminder.
6. **Keep both for a week.** Your old numbers stay in HabitKit. In HabitForge every rope [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md) on your first check-in, and that first week is when it looks thinnest.
