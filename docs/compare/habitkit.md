# Moving from HabitKit to HabitForge

**HabitKit is a grid you fill one tile a day, with no account, no feed and nobody watching. HabitForge needs a Google sign-in and puts other people beside you. It is a different kind of quiet.**

## How quiet you want it

If nobody watching is the point, stay. A new HabitForge profile is public and ranked until you change it in Settings.

Come over if other people are what keep you going, you want a web app as well, and you want the habit’s cue, routine and reward written down beside the rope. Like HabitKit’s grid, the rope keeps the days you kept after a miss. Unlike it, there is no widget ([other gaps](./index.md#what-habitforge-does-not-do)).

## Bringing your habits

1. **Export a backup from HabitKit.** HabitForge cannot read it. Keep it anyway; it holds your history.
2. **[Sign in with Google](../getting-started.md).**
3. **Decide how public you want to be, before anything else.** In Settings, [switch off the public profile and leaderboard](../features/community.md) if you want it as quiet as HabitKit.
4. **Re-add each habit.** One you are quitting is a **break** habit. [Create a habit](../habits/create.md).
5. **Set a [daily reminder](../features/reminders-and-notifications.md)** if the grid on your home screen was your reminder. The widget does not come across.
6. **Look at the rope after a week, not a day.** It [starts as a thread](../habits/rope-strength.md).
