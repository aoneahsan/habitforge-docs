# Delete your HabitForge account and data

**HabitForge account deletion is a permanent, email-based request that erases your account and personal data, handled through the dedicated [account-deletion page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/account-deletion).** It isn't a silent one-click toggle: you send a deletion request by email, verify your identity, and receive a confirmation once the erasure is complete. This deliberate, verified flow exists so your data can't be wiped by accident or by someone who isn't you. The page is GDPR- and CCPA-aware, spells out exactly what gets deleted versus what may be retained for legal reasons, and recommends exporting your data first. Deletion cannot be undone, so read this before you start.

## Use cases

### Leaving HabitForge for good

If you've decided to stop using the app, deletion removes your account, your habits, your progress, and your settings — a clean, permanent exit rather than just signing out.

### Exercising your right to erasure

Under GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure) and CCPA, you can require deletion of your personal data. The account-deletion flow is how HabitForge honours that request.

### Clearing your data before handing off a device

If you're done with the app on a shared or retired device, deleting the account ensures your personal habit data doesn't linger in the account behind it.

## How to use it

1. Open the [account-deletion page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/account-deletion) directly, or in the app go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**, which routes you there.
2. (Recommended) Export your data first — use the in-app [export](./export), or the page's **Request Data Export** option for a portable copy.
3. **Submit the request:** send the pre-filled deletion email (subject "Account Deletion Request"), including the email address on your account.
4. **Verify your identity:** click the link in the verification email sent to your registered address.
5. **Processing:** the request is processed within the published window, after which your personal data is permanently erased.
6. **Confirmation:** you receive a final email once deletion is complete.

## What gets deleted

When deletion completes, the following are permanently removed: your **Account** details (email, display name, password hash, profile picture), all your **Habits** (names, descriptions, cues, routines, rewards), your **Progress** (completion records, habit-strength metrics, milestones), your **Settings** (preferences, notification settings, theme choices), personal **Analytics** linked to your account, and any account-linked **Support** tickets.

## What may be retained

For legal compliance, a small amount of non-identifying or legally required data may be kept after deletion: **anonymized aggregate analytics** (retained indefinitely, not tied to you), **transaction records** where applicable (retained ~7 years for financial compliance), and **communication logs** of support correspondence (retained ~3 years). None of these identify you the way your deleted account data did.

## FAQ

### Can I delete my account with one click inside the app?

No. The in-app **Delete Account** button takes you to the account-deletion page and starts an email-based request that includes identity verification. The verification step is intentional — it protects you from accidental or unauthorized deletion.

### How long does deletion take?

The page lists the timeline: your request is acknowledged within about 48 hours, identity verification takes roughly 1–3 days, and data-deletion processing completes within the window published on the deletion page, followed by a final confirmation email.

### Is deletion really permanent?

Yes. It's permanent and irreversible — once your data is erased it can't be recovered. That's why exporting your data first is strongly recommended.

### What if I just have a problem, not a reason to leave?

The deletion page offers a **Contact Support** option before you delete. If something isn't working, reaching out may solve it without losing your account and history.

### Can I get my data before it's deleted?

Yes. Use the self-serve [export](./export) in Settings, or the page's **Request Data Export** to receive a portable copy. Do this before submitting the deletion request, since deletion is final.

### What's the legal basis for this?

HabitForge processes deletions under GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure) and CCPA, and commits to honouring legitimate requests within the legally required timeframe.
