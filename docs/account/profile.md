# Your HabitForge account, profile, and settings

**In HabitForge, your account and profile are managed from the Settings page — there is no separate "profile" screen; Settings is the one place where your identity, appearance, notifications, privacy, data, and account actions all live.** Your account identity (email and display name) is created when you [sign up](../getting-started/sign-up), and from Settings you control how the app looks, how it notifies you, what you share, and what happens to your account. Everything you change is saved to your account and synced across web and mobile, so your preferences follow you between devices. This page explains what each Settings section does and which actions are reversible.

## Use cases

### Making the app look the way you want

The Theme Customizer in Settings controls appearance (light/dark and accent), so HabitForge matches your taste and reduces eye strain. Theme choices save to your account and apply everywhere you sign in.

### Controlling notifications

The Notifications section lets you toggle email notifications, push notifications, and habit reminders independently. Enabling push asks for your device's permission first, and a **Test Notification** button lets you confirm it actually fires before you rely on it.

### Deciding what you share

Two privacy toggles — **Share Progress** (let friends see your habit progress) and **Public Profile** (make your profile visible to other users) — are both off-by-default choices you control. Nothing about your activity is shared unless you switch these on.

### Managing the account itself

The Account section is where you reset all settings to their defaults, sign out, or start account deletion. These are the "big" actions, grouped together so they're easy to find and hard to trigger by accident.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar (or in the [live app](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com)).
2. Use the **Theme Customizer** card to set appearance and accent; changes save automatically (a "Saved" badge confirms).
3. In **Notifications**, toggle email, push, and reminders. For push, accept the device permission prompt, then optionally tap **Test Notification**.
4. In **Privacy**, set **Share Progress** and **Public Profile** to match what you want others to see.
5. Use **Export Data** to download your habits and history — see [Export your data](./export) for the full breakdown.
6. In **Account**, choose **Reset to Default Settings**, **Delete Account**, or **Sign Out** as needed.

## FAQ

### Is there a dedicated profile page?

No. HabitForge keeps account and profile management on the single **Settings** page rather than splitting it across multiple screens. Your display name and email come from sign-up; everything else — appearance, notifications, privacy, data, and account actions — is configured in Settings.

### What does "Reset to Default Settings" change?

It restores your preferences (theme, notifications, privacy toggles) to their defaults. It's a settings reset, not a data wipe — your habits, completions, and history are untouched. It does ask for confirmation first, and the reset itself can't be undone.

### What's the difference between "Share Progress" and "Public Profile"?

**Share Progress** lets friends see your habit progress, while **Public Profile** makes your profile visible to other users. They're separate switches, both off by default, so you can share one without the other.

### Do my settings sync across devices?

Yes. Settings save to your account, so signing in on another device restores your theme, notification, and privacy choices. See [Account basics](../getting-started/account-basics) for how cross-device sync works.

### Some sections aren't showing for me — why?

A few sections (such as notifications and data export) are feature-gated and only appear when enabled for the app. If you don't see one, it isn't active in your build; the core account actions are always available.

### How do I close my account?

Use **Delete Account** in the Account section, which takes you to the account-deletion flow. Read [Account deletion](./deletion) first — deletion is permanent, and exporting your data beforehand is recommended.
