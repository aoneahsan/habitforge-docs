# Wellness dashboard and 7-day wellness score

**The HabitForge Wellness Dashboard rolls your last 7 days of tracking into one weighted 0–100 wellness score, plus eight category scores for habits, sleep, hydration, exercise, mindfulness, mood, productivity, and nutrition.** It reads whatever you've already logged across the app — completions, sleep, water, workouts, meditation and breathing, mood, focus and Pomodoro sessions, and meals — and turns it into a single circular gauge with a plain-language label. Below the gauge, each category gets its own percentage and a one-line detail. It's a motivational overview, not a medical assessment: the dashboard carries an in-app reminder that it is **not professional health advice**.

## Use cases

### A single number that reflects the whole week

Instead of opening eight different trackers, you open one page and see "your week" as a score out of 100 with a label like Good or Excellent. It's a fast, low-effort weekly check-in that captures whether you broadly showed up across the areas you care about.

### Finding the weak link

The eight category bars make it obvious where the score is being dragged down. If Sleep sits at 30% while everything else is green, you know exactly what to work on next — no spreadsheet required.

### Rewarding the act of tracking

Each category only contributes when there's data behind it, so logging more areas tends to produce a fuller, fairer picture. The "Track More for Better Insights" note nudges you to capture sleep, mood, water, and exercise for a more complete score.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Wellness Dashboard** (the Wellness page) from the sidebar, or in the [live app](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com).
2. Read the **Wellness Score** gauge in the centre — a 0–100 number with a colour and a label (Getting Started → Fair → Good → Excellent).
3. Scan the eight **category cards** (Habits, Sleep, Hydration, Exercise, Mindfulness, Mood, Productivity, Nutrition); each shows a percentage, a progress bar, and a detail line.
4. Read the **Insights** section for short, rule-based suggestions based on this week's numbers.
5. To move the score, log more in the relevant tracker — for example the [water tracker](../trackers/water), [sleep tracker](../trackers/sleep), or [mood tracker](../trackers/mood) — then revisit the page.

## FAQ

### What time window does the score cover?

The dashboard looks at the **last 7 days** only. It's a rolling weekly summary, so it always reflects your recent week rather than your all-time history. For long-range patterns, use the [calendar heatmap](./calendar-heatmap) instead.

### How is the overall score weighted?

The eight categories are blended with fixed weights — habit completion carries the most weight, followed by sleep and exercise, then hydration, mindfulness, mood, and productivity, with nutrition weighted lightest. Each category is itself a 0–100 measure of your last 7 days (for example, exercise rewards a few workouts a week, and mindfulness rewards weekly meditation and breathing minutes).

### Are the insights medical advice?

No. The insights are simple rule-based messages triggered by your own numbers (for example, a note when sleep quality is low), and the page shows a health compliance disclaimer. **HabitForge does not provide medical advice.** For health decisions, consult a qualified professional.

### Why is a category at 0%?

Because there's nothing logged for it in the last 7 days. Categories only score when there's data behind them, so an untouched tracker reads 0% until you start logging it.

### Does the hydration category use my water goal?

The dashboard's hydration category measures your recent water logging against a general daily reference rather than reading your personal goal from the water tracker. Your custom goal still lives on the [water tracker](../trackers/water) page; the wellness score is a broad-strokes summary, not a precise audit of each tracker's own targets.

### Is the wellness dashboard free?

Yes — it's part of the free, zero-cost app, with no paid tier. It simply summarises data you're already logging in HabitForge.
