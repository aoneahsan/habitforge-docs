# Guided breathing exercises with paced patterns

**The HabitForge breathing screen walks you through a fixed breathing pattern with an animated circle that grows as you inhale and shrinks as you exhale.** You pick one of five built-in patterns, press start, and follow along: a large label tells you the current phase ("Breathe In", "Hold", "Breathe Out"), a number counts down the seconds left in that phase, and a counter shows which cycle you are on. The circle's size and timing match the pattern exactly, so your breath has something steady to pace against. When you finish all the cycles, the session is saved to your history. It is a calming, free personal tool — **not** a medical device, and the app shows that reminder.

## Use cases

### Calming down before a stressful moment

Before a call, an exam, or a hard conversation, run a few rounds of **Box Breathing** (4-4-4-4). The equal counts and visible circle give your attention a simple job and slow your breath down without you having to count in your head.

### Winding down for sleep

The **4-7-8 Relaxing** pattern uses a long 8-second exhale, the in-app description credits it to Dr. Andrew Weil's relaxation technique. **Deep Relaxation** (4-4-8) is another long-exhale option meant for bedtime. Run a few cycles as part of a wind-down routine.

### A quick energy lift

Feeling sluggish? **Energizing Breath** is a fast 2-2 rhythm over ten cycles to wake you up, the opposite intent of the calming patterns. It is short by design.

## How to use it

1. Open the breathing screen at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/breathing](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/breathing) while signed in.
2. Under **Choose a Pattern**, tap one of the five cards. Each shows its timing (e.g. `4-4-4-4`), a short description, and its default number of cycles.
3. Press **Start**. Follow the animated circle and the on-screen phase label and countdown.
4. Watch the **Cycle X of N** counter to see how far through the pattern you are.
5. Let it run to the last cycle to complete and save the session, or press **Stop** to end early.
6. Review **Recent Sessions** for the pattern name, cycles, date, and total seconds of each completed session.

## FAQ

### What are the five patterns and their timing?

Box Breathing (4-4-4-4, 4 cycles) for calm focus; 4-7-8 Relaxing (inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8, 4 cycles) for relaxation and sleep; Calm Breathing (inhale 4, exhale 6, 6 cycles) for a longer-exhale reset; Energizing Breath (2-2, 10 cycles) for alertness; and Deep Relaxation (4-4-8, 5 cycles) for bedtime. The "Hold" phases are skipped when a pattern sets them to zero.

### Can I create my own custom pattern?

Not on this screen. The five patterns are fixed presets — you cannot edit the counts or add a new pattern in the current UI. Pick the preset closest to what you want.

### Does stopping early save the session?

No. Only a session you let run all the way to the final cycle is recorded. If you press **Stop** partway through, nothing is saved for that attempt. So your history reflects fully completed rounds only.

### Is there sound or voice guidance?

No. The pacing is fully visual and numeric — the growing/shrinking circle, the phase label, and the per-phase countdown. There is no audio cue, so keep the screen in view while you breathe.

### Is this medical advice?

No. These are general calming and focus exercises for personal use. They do not treat any condition and are not a substitute for professional care. If you feel light-headed, stop and breathe normally; if you have a respiratory or heart condition, check with a healthcare provider first.
