# Deep focus timer with distraction tracking

**The HabitForge Deep Focus timer runs one long, uninterrupted work session and scores how focused it was based on how long you stayed and how often you got distracted.** Unlike the Pomodoro loop, this is a single block: you pick a length from 30 to 240 minutes, optionally label what you are working on, and start. While it runs you tap a button each time something pulls you away. When the block ends — or when you reset it — the timer saves the session with a focus score from 0 to 100, then totals your deep-work hours, sessions, and average score for the day. It is a personal-awareness tool, free to use, with no website or app blocking built into this screen.

## Use cases

### A single long stretch of deep work

For writing, coding, studying, or design, one 90-minute block beats four scattered half-hours. Pick the 90 preset, label it `Draft report`, and start. The ring and remaining-time readout keep you honest about how much of the block is left.

### Measuring how distracted you really are

Each time you check your phone, switch tabs, or get pulled into a chat, tap the red "I got distracted" button. The running count and live focus score turn a vague feeling ("I kept getting interrupted") into a number you can compare across days.

### Building a deep-work habit

Pair the timer with a habit such as `One distraction-free hour`. The "Today's Deep Work" card shows total hours and average score, which is a cleaner signal of progress than just marking the habit done. See [Habits → Create a habit](../habits/create).

## How to use it

1. Open the Deep Focus timer at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/focus](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/focus) while signed in.
2. Choose a duration: tap a preset (30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes) or type a **custom** value between 5 and 240 minutes and press **Apply**.
3. Optionally add a **Session Label** (up to 100 characters) describing what you are doing.
4. Press **Start**. Use **Pause** to stop the clock without ending the session.
5. Whenever you lose focus, tap the red distraction button. Your distraction count and live focus score update on screen.
6. Let the timer reach zero to complete the session, or press **Reset** to stop early — both save the session (Reset marks it incomplete).
7. Review the **Today's Deep Work** card for total hours, session count, average score, and your recent sessions.

## FAQ

### How is the focus score calculated?

The score rewards finishing the block and penalises distractions. It is the time you completed (actual ÷ planned, weighted to 80) plus a 20-point base, minus 5 points per distraction, clamped to 0–100. So a full session with no distractions lands at 100; finishing fully but tapping the distraction button four times lands around 80. Scores of 70+ show green, 40–69 amber, and below 40 red.

### Does this timer block websites or apps?

No. This screen does not block anything — the distraction button is a **self-report** you tap yourself. If you want actual site blocking, that lives in the separate HabitForge browser extension's Focus Mode, not in this timer.

### What happens if I press Reset before the timer ends?

If the session had started, Reset saves it as an **incomplete** session, using however much time elapsed and the distractions recorded so far, then clears the screen for a fresh block. Nothing you tracked is thrown away.

### Does the label do anything besides show on screen?

It is saved with the session and appears in your recent-sessions list, so you can tell at a glance which block was `Draft report` versus `Inbox zero`. It is optional — an unlabeled session is stored as "Untitled session".

### Is there a sound when the block finishes?

No audible alert plays in this screen; the session simply completes and the stats update. Keep the tab in view if you want to catch the moment it ends.
