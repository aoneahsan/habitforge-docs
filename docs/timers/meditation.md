# Meditation timer with mood check and breathing

**The HabitForge meditation timer is a quiet sit-timer with a gently pulsing breathing circle and a before-and-after mood check.** You pick a session type and a length, rate how you feel, then start. A circle expands and contracts on a four-second rhythm with "Breathe In" / "Breathe Out" cues while the time counts down (or counts up, for an open-ended sit). When you finish, you rate your mood again and save the session, so over time your history shows how a sit tends to move your mood. It is a personal mindfulness aid, free to use — **not** medical or psychological treatment, and the app shows that reminder on the page.

## Use cases

### A short reset between tasks

Five or ten minutes is enough to break a stressful afternoon. Choose the 5- or 10-minute timer, rate your mood, and follow the breathing circle. The post-session mood check often shows the shift you would otherwise not notice.

### An open-ended morning sit

If you prefer to sit without a fixed end, pick **Open-ended**. The timer counts up instead of down, so you stop when you are ready rather than racing a clock. Your elapsed time is still recorded.

### Tracking what actually helps your mood

Because every session stores a mood-before and mood-after emoji, the recent-sessions list becomes a simple log of which sits left you feeling better. Pair it with the [Mood tracker](../trackers/mood) for a fuller picture across the day.

## How to use it

1. Open the meditation timer at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/meditation](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/meditation) while signed in.
2. Pick a **Session Type**: Timed, Open-ended, Body Scan, or Loving-kindness.
3. For a timed type, choose a **Duration** card — 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes.
4. Answer **"How are you feeling now?"** by tapping a mood emoji. You must select one before you can start.
5. Press **Start**. Follow the breathing circle; use **Pause/Resume** or **Stop** as needed.
6. When the timer ends (or you stop), rate **"How do you feel after meditating?"** and press **Save Session**.
7. Check **Recent Sessions** to see your session type, length, date, and the mood shift for each sit.

## FAQ

### Do the session types include guided audio?

No. Body Scan and Loving-kindness are **labels** that get saved with the session — the on-screen experience is the same breathing circle and timer for every type. There is no voice narration or guided audio track. Use the type as your own intention for the sit, not as a guided lesson.

### Why do I have to pick a mood before I can start?

The before/after mood check is the core of this feature, so a starting mood is required. The post-session mood is also required before you can save. If you would rather not log mood at all, this screen will not fit — it is built around that comparison.

### Does "Total meditation time" cover my whole history?

It reflects the most recent sessions loaded on the page (the latest seven), not your entire all-time history. So the total and the recent list are a rolling recent view, which is enough to spot a pattern but is not a lifetime sum.

### Is the breathing circle a specific technique?

It is a simple, even in-and-out pace (about four seconds each way) meant to settle your attention, not a clinical protocol. For structured patterns like box breathing or 4-7-8, use the dedicated [Breathing exercises](./breathing) screen.

### Is this medical advice?

No. HabitForge is a habit and wellness tool for personal awareness. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace care from a qualified professional. If you are dealing with anxiety, depression, or any health concern, talk to a healthcare provider.
