# Questions

## Do I get the code with a licence?

No. A licence is a hosted copy that we run. The code changes hands only with a [buyout](./delivered.md#a-buyout).

## Can someone else license HabitForge too?

Yes, until somebody buys the source. After a buyout we stop selling it, and licences sold before then keep their terms.

## Is there anything to pay after a buyout?

Yes, a royalty: 7% of the gross revenue HabitForge earns, not counting sales tax or VAT you collect, paid every quarter with a revenue statement for as long as it earns revenue, including after a resale.

## How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our Wise account. The payment page shows the details and asks for the transfer reference. Once we confirm the transfer, the licence key arrives by email.

## What happens when the six months of updates end?

Your copy keeps running on the last version it received. A [renewal](./updates.md) of three, six or twelve months brings the updates back.

## Who pays for the domain and the store account?

You do. Your domain, a Google Play developer account and any paid service you choose stay in your name.

## Can you change it for us?

Yes, as [custom work](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work): a points rule that follows your programme, a link to your booking system, a screen nobody else needs. It is quoted and paid separately from the licence.
