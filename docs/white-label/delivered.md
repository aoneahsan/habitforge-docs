# What is delivered

**A licence covers the platforms you pay for, run by us under your name.** HabitForge ships two platforms today: the web app and the Android app. There is no browser extension, so there is none to license.

## The web app

The whole of HabitForge in the browser, on the domain you choose (for example `habits.yourgym.com`): habits and check-ins, the rope, analytics, achievements, the community, challenges and the leaderboard. It installs to the home screen and works offline, as the main site does.

## The mobile app

An Android build under your name and icon, published through a Google Play developer account in your name. iOS is part of the same platform and is delivered once you provide or fund an Apple developer account; until then your iPhone and iPad members use the web app.

## What stays with us

The source code, the hosting and the release process. We deploy each HabitForge release to your copy for as long as your [updates](./updates.md) run. Your domain, your Play account and any paid service you choose stay in your name, and their costs are yours.

## A buyout

A buyout hands over the source code of every repository that builds the platforms HabitForge ships:

- **The app**: the web app and the Android project, which build from one repository, with their build and release setup.
- **The database**: the Postgres schema, its row-level security rules and the server functions, as migrations you apply to a project of your own.
- **The help site**: this documentation, ready to publish on your own domain.

It comes with six months of updates. From then on HabitForge is off sale to everyone else; anyone who licensed it before you keeps their terms.

A buyout also carries a royalty: you pay us 7% of the gross revenue HabitForge earns, not counting sales tax or VAT you collect, every quarter with a revenue statement. It runs for as long as HabitForge earns revenue, and it is still owed after a resale. The [licence terms](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms#lt-buyout) say the same.
