# White-label and licensing

**A coach, a gym, a workplace or a club can run HabitForge under their own name.** We host the copy, connect your domain, put your name, logo and colours on every screen, and keep it updated. Your members check in and watch their ropes thicken there, as they would on HabitForge itself.

A licence is not the source code. It is a hosted copy that we deploy and run. The code changes hands only if you [buy it outright](./delivered.md#a-buyout).

Prices and the total for the platforms you pick are on [the white-label pricing page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing). This section explains what you get, in what order, and how to check a licence.

## Two ways to pay

- **A licence**, priced per platform: the web app, the Android and iOS app, or both. Two platforms together cost less than two bought apart. We may license HabitForge to other buyers until somebody buys it outright.
- **A buyout**: every repository that builds HabitForge handed over, with six months of updates. After that we sell it to nobody else, and a 7% royalty on its gross revenue is paid to us quarterly for as long as it earns.

Changes made only for your copy are [custom work](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted and paid separately.
