# Updates and renewals

**Six months of updates come with every licence and every buyout, counted from the day of payment.** An update is a new HabitForge release: fixes, new screens, a change to how the rope is drawn. We deploy each one to your copy while your update period runs.

## When the six months end

Nothing stops working. Your copy stays on the last version it received, and your members keep checking in as before.

## Renewing

A renewal adds three, six or twelve months. It is counted from the later of today and the day your current updates end, so renewing early never loses time. Renew on [the updates page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email the licence was issued to; the prices are there too.

A buyer who bought the source can renew the same way.
