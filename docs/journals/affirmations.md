# Daily affirmations library and custom list

**The HabitForge affirmations screen is a library of 200+ positive statements with an "affirmation of the day", favorites, category filters, and your own custom additions.** A featured affirmation sits at the top, chosen by the calendar date. Below it you can write your own affirmation, then browse the full built-in library — grouped into Self-Love, Confidence, Health, Success, Gratitude, Growth, and Abundance. Tap the heart to favorite the lines that resonate, filter to **Favorites Only**, or jump between categories with tabs. Custom affirmations you add are saved to your account and marked as yours. It is a simple, free reading-and-collecting tool with no subscription.

## Use cases

### A daily one-line boost

Open the screen and read the **affirmation of the day** at the top. It is picked by the date, so it is the same line for that whole day and refreshes the next day — a tiny, consistent prompt to start with a positive note.

### Building a personal favorites set

Skim the library, heart the affirmations that actually land for you, and switch on **Favorites Only**. You end up with a short, personal collection instead of wading through 200 lines every time.

### Writing affirmations in your own words

Generic lines do not work for everyone. Use **Create Your Own** to write an affirmation in your language, pick a category, and add it. Your custom lines are saved, favorited automatically, and tagged "Custom" so they stand out.

## How to use it

1. Open affirmations at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/affirmations](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/affirmations) while signed in.
2. Read the **Affirmation of the Day** card at the top; use **Share** to send or copy it.
3. To add your own, type it under **Create Your Own**, choose a category, and press **Add Affirmation**.
4. Browse the **Affirmation Library**: tap a category tab to filter, or toggle **Favorites Only**.
5. Tap the **heart** on any affirmation to favorite or unfavorite it; tap **Share** to share that line.

## FAQ

### How is the "affirmation of the day" chosen?

It is selected from the built-in library using the calendar date, so everyone sees the same line on a given day and it changes once per day. It is deterministic — not random per visit — which is why reloading the page keeps the same daily affirmation.

### What happens when I favorite a built-in affirmation?

Favoriting saves a small record to your account so the line shows up under Favorites Only and keeps its heart. Unfavoriting a built-in line removes that record. Your custom affirmations are favorited by default when you create them.

### Does the Share button always open a share sheet?

It uses your device's native share option when one is available, and otherwise copies the affirmation text to your clipboard so you can paste it anywhere. On some desktop browsers that means it copies rather than opening a share dialog.

### Will the app remind me of my affirmations?

Not from this screen — it is a page you open, with no built-in push reminder for affirmations. If you want a daily nudge, pair it with a habit and a reminder. See [Habits → Schedule and reminders](../habits/schedule-reminders).

### Are these affirmations professional advice?

No. They are general positive statements for personal encouragement, not therapy or medical advice. They are a self-reflection aid, nothing more.
