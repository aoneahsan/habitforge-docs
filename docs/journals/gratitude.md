# Gratitude journal with three daily entries

**The HabitForge gratitude journal is a focused daily practice: write up to three things you are grateful for, tag each with a category, and keep a streak of consecutive days.** Each day you get three input rows and a rotating prompt to spark ideas. Fill in one, two, or all three, choose a category for each (People, Health, Nature, Work, Learning, or Other), and save. A flame counter at the top shows how many days in a row you have logged gratitude, and your recent days appear below. Saving again the same day updates that day's entry rather than adding a second one. It is a small, free habit designed to take under a minute.

## Use cases

### A one-minute end-of-day ritual

Three lines is the whole point — it is short enough to do every night without dreading it. Note the good moments, tag them, and save. The brevity is what makes it stick where longer journaling often does not.

### Keeping a visible streak going

The consecutive-day streak turns the practice into a light game: miss a day and it resets. Many people find the streak, more than any single entry, is what keeps them coming back. Pair it with a habit like `Three gratitudes` for a second nudge — see [Habits → Create a habit](../habits/create).

### Noticing what you are grateful for most

Because each item carries a category, scanning your recent entries shows whether your gratitude clusters around People, Health, Work, or somewhere else — a quiet insight you get for free just by tagging.

## How to use it

1. Open the gratitude journal at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/gratitude](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/gratitude) while signed in.
2. Read the **prompt** at the top for inspiration; tap the refresh icon for a different one.
3. In each of the three rows, type something you are grateful for and pick a **category** from the dropdown.
4. Press **Save Gratitude**. You need at least one filled row; empty rows are simply ignored.
5. Check the **flame counter** for your current consecutive-day streak.
6. Scroll to **Recent Entries** to review the last several days with their items and category tags.

## FAQ

### Do I have to fill in all three?

No. Three rows are offered, but you can save with just one filled in — only the rows that have text are stored. Three is the suggested target, not a requirement.

### How does the streak work?

It counts back from today (or yesterday) through consecutive days that each have an entry. Miss a day and the streak resets to zero the next time it is calculated. The streak is worked out from your recent entries each time the page loads.

### Can I add a fourth item?

Not in this screen — it gives exactly three rows. If you have more, combine related thoughts into one line or rotate them across days. The three-item format is intentional to keep the practice quick.

### Can I edit a past day's gratitude?

You can update **today's** entry by changing the rows and saving again. The Recent Entries list below is a read-only review of earlier days — there is no in-place edit for previous days in this screen.

### Is this the same as the daily journal?

No. The [daily journal](./daily-journal) is open-ended long-form writing; gratitude is a structured, three-item, one-minute practice with a streak. Many people use both — a quick gratitude log plus a longer journal entry when they have more to say.
