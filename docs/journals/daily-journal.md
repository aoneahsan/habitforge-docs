# Daily journaling with prompts and templates

**The HabitForge daily journal gives you one dated entry per day to write freely, with optional templates, a writing-prompt button, and a live word count.** Open it, pick Free Write, Morning, or Evening, and start typing in a plain text area. Morning and Evening drop a ready-made reflection scaffold into the box; Free Write starts blank. If you are stuck, tap for a random prompt drawn from a built-in set of reflective questions. Saving stores the entry against today's date — write again later the same day and it updates that single entry rather than making a second one. Your last several entries appear below, and you can open any of them full-screen. It is a private, free writing space with no formatting frills.

## Use cases

### A morning intention-setting page

Select the **Morning** template and you get prompts for gratitude, your top three priorities, and how you want to feel today. Fill them in before the day starts and you have a one-screen plan plus a record you can look back on.

### An evening wind-down reflection

The **Evening** template asks what went well, what could have gone better, what you learned, and what you are looking forward to. It is a low-effort way to close the day and notice patterns over a week.

### Free-writing to clear your head

When something is on your mind, pick **Free Write**, tap a prompt if you want a starting point, and just type. The live word count quietly rewards getting words down, and the entry is saved privately to your account.

## How to use it

1. Open the journal at [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/journal](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/journal) while signed in.
2. Choose a template tab: **Free Write**, **Morning**, or **Evening**. Morning/Evening insert a scaffold you can edit or delete.
3. Optionally tap **Get Writing Prompt** for a random reflective question; tap again for a new one.
4. Add an optional **Title**, then write in the main text area. The word count updates as you type.
5. Press **Save Entry**. If you have already written today, the button reads **Update Entry** and edits the same entry.
6. Scroll to **Past Entries** and click any card to read the full entry in a pop-up.

## FAQ

### Can I write more than one entry in a single day?

No. The journal is keyed to the date, so there is one entry per day. Saving again the same day updates that entry instead of creating a new one. If you want to add to it later, just reopen the journal and keep writing — your earlier text is loaded back in.

### How many past entries can I see?

The Past Entries list shows your most recent entries (the latest ten). Older entries still exist in your account, but this screen displays the recent set rather than your full archive.

### Is there rich text — bold, headings, images?

No. The editor is a plain text area. Line breaks and spacing are preserved when you read an entry back, but there is no bold/italic formatting, no headings, and no image or file attachments. Templates are just pre-typed text you can change.

### Do I have to use a prompt or template?

No. Prompts and templates are optional helpers. Free Write with no prompt is a perfectly valid blank page. The only requirement to save is that the entry has some text.

### Is my journal private?

Yes. Entries are saved to your own account and are not shared. You can export your data from your profile, and deleting your account removes your entries. See [Getting started → Privacy basics](../getting-started/privacy-basics).
