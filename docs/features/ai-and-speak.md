# AI and Speak

Four features use AI, each sending Anthropic — or OpenAI, when our Anthropic credit is used up — only what that request needs. An AI draft can be wrong. Nothing
reaches your habits, check-ins or the community until you press Save or Post.

## What the four features do

- **Habit drafts.** On a new habit, Draft with AI fills the cue, routine, reward, craving and investment from
  the habit's name. Put back what I had restores what you wrote.
- **Weekly reflections.** The dashboard reads your last seven finished days back, habit by habit, with one
  suggestion.
- **Post wording.** On the community board, Help me word this rewords a post you wrote. It never writes one
  from nothing.
- **Speak.** Speak listens to what you did and what you want to start, then suggests records for you to check.

## What can Speak do?

One recording can suggest three kinds of record: check-ins for habits you did today or yesterday, a new habit
with its cue, routine and reward, and a community post. Nothing is saved until you press Save. Change or skip
any suggestion first.

## How do I start?

Press Speak, then Start recording. The microphone is asked for only then. Recordings run up to 2 minutes.

## What are Spark, Pulse and Surge?

The AI levels. Spark is on Free. Pulse is on Pro. Surge is on Family, and on Pro after three unbroken paid
months. Settings → AI shows your level and this month's use, which comes back on the 1st. The allowance for
each feature is on the [plans page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/pricing).

## Where do my recordings go, and for how long?

Settings → Your recordings: play, read or delete. The audio is deleted after 30 days; the words and
suggestions stay until you delete the recording or your account.

Who processes what, and the full detail, is in the
[privacy policy](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/privacy#ai).
