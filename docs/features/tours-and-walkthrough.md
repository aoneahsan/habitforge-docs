# Page tours and How HabitForge works

**HabitForge shows you around in two ways: a short tour of the page you are on, and one page that explains the whole app in six steps.** Both are there whenever you want them, and neither starts by itself.

## The page tour

Nine pages have a tour: the landing page, Today, Habits, New habit, Analytics, Achievements, Community, Settings and Plans.

- **Start it** from the signpost button, *Take a tour of this page*. It sits beside the theme button in the header, on every page that has a tour, on a phone too.
- **The first time** you open one of those pages, a short line under the button says *New here? Take a quick tour of this page.* It shows once per page, and once for your account, so a second device does not repeat it. It leaves by itself after a few seconds.
- **Move through it** with *Back* and *Next*. *All steps* lets you jump to any step, and *Skip tour* or Escape closes it at any point.
- **Share a step.** The address carries the step (`?tour=3`), so a link opens the tour at that step.

On a phone the step card sits at the bottom of the screen, and the part of the page it explains is scrolled into view.

## How HabitForge works

[How HabitForge works](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/how-habitforge-works) is the whole app in six steps, one real screen each:

1. **Create a habit**: name it, pick build, break or maintain, and write the loop.
2. **Check in each day**: one press on Today, and press it again to undo.
3. **Watch the rope thicken**: thread, string, rope, cable, chain.
4. **Keep going with other people**: the board, friends, challenges and the leaderboard.
5. **Say it with Speak**: say what you did, and nothing is saved until you press Save.
6. **Pick a plan when you need one**: Free is the whole product; Pro and Family lift the ceilings.

It is open to everyone, signed in or not. Find it under **How it works** in the sidebar's foot, in the site footer, and on the [sitemap](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/sitemap). The address carries the step too (`?step=3`), and the arrow keys and a swipe move between steps.
