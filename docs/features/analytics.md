# Analytics

**Analytics turn your check-in history into a weekly picture of how your habits are doing.** Instead of a single number, you get charts that show the shape of your consistency over time.

## What you'll see

- **Weekly summaries** of check-ins across your habits.
- **Trends** that make a slipping habit visible before the rope frays.
- **Per-habit history** so you can tell which habits are holding and which need attention.

Charts are drawn with D3 and read directly from your own check-in data — nothing is estimated or inferred about you.

## Tips

- **Look weekly, act daily.** The weekly view spots patterns; the daily check-in fixes them.
- **Compare habits.** If one habit is dragging, its trend line usually shows it first.
- **Use it after a slip.** Analytics make it obvious when a habit is recovering.
