# Levels and points

**Points are the score you build across every habit; your level is the tier those points put you in.** Check-ins earn points, achievements add bonus points, and as your total grows you climb a ten-step forge ladder.

## The forge ladder

There are ten tiers, from your first check-in to a long-running practice:

| Level | Tier |
|---|---|
| 1 | Spark |
| 2 | Kindling |
| 3 | Ember |
| 4 | Flame |
| 5 | Forge Hand |
| 6 | Apprentice Smith |
| 7 | Journeyman |
| 8 | Blacksmith |
| 9 | Master Smith |
| 10 | Forge Master |

Early tiers come quickly so progress feels real in the first week; later tiers widen so the top stays meaningful. Your **points are the source of truth** — every screen computes your level from them, so a level is simply the tier your current point total falls into.

## Where you see it

- A **level tile** on the dashboard shows your points, current tier, and progress to the next.
- A **level badge** appears on your profile and, if you join the [community](../features/community.md), on leaderboard rows.

## Tips

- **Points reward consistency, not intensity.** Many small check-ins outpace a single burst.
- **Achievements are a shortcut.** Unlocking one adds bonus points — see [Achievements](../features/achievements.md).
- **The number is a nudge, not the goal.** The rope tells you how a habit is doing; the level tells you how far you've come overall.
