# Check-ins and streaks

**A check-in is how you tell HabitForge you did a habit.** Each day a habit is due, you mark it done from the dashboard. Consecutive check-ins build a **streak**, and every check-in adds **points**.

## How it works

1. Open the dashboard. Habits due today sit at the top with a check button.
2. Tap the button to check in for that habit.
3. Your streak for the habit goes up, points are added, and the [rope](./rope-strength.md) grows a little stronger.
4. Miss a due day and the streak resets — the rope starts to fray.

Streaks count consecutive on-schedule days, so a habit scheduled for weekdays is not broken by skipping a weekend it was not due.

## Your longest streak is kept

A broken streak resets the one you are running. It does **not** erase the longest one you have ever reached —
that is kept separately and shown on your dashboard and on the leaderboard.

It only ever goes up. Missing a day costs you the current run and nothing else, which is the point: the
number that says what you are capable of should not be deleted by one bad week.

## Points and level

Check-ins earn points that accumulate across all your habits. Points raise your forge **level** through ten tiers — see [Levels and points](./levels-and-points.md).

## Tips

- **Check in at the same time each day.** A fixed moment (right after your cue) makes the check-in itself a habit.
- **Do not backfill dishonestly.** The rope is only useful when it reflects what really happened.
- **A reset is not a failure.** Streaks are meant to restart; the level and total points you have earned stay with you.
