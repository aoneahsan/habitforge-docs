# Get started

**Getting started with HabitForge takes two steps: open the app, then sign in with Google.** There is no separate account to create and no password to remember.

## Open the app

- **On the web:** go to [habitforge.aoneahsan.com](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com) in any modern browser. It works on desktop and mobile.
- **On Android:** the native build is finished and in internal testing, so it is not on Google Play yet. Until the listing goes live, open the web app in Chrome and [add it to your home screen](./apps/install-and-offline.md) — it installs and works offline. See [The Android app](./apps/android.md) for what the native build adds.

There is no iOS app yet. On an iPhone or iPad, use the web app in Safari and [add it to your home screen](./apps/install-and-offline.md).

## Sign in

**HabitForge uses Google sign-in only.** On the web this is a Google popup; on Android it uses the native account picker. There is no email-and-password option, no Apple sign-in, and no anonymous mode.

1. Click **Continue with Google**.
2. Pick the Google account you want to use.
3. You land on the dashboard, ready to add a habit.

Your habits, streaks and settings sync to that Google account, so signing in on another device restores everything, down to the ten appearance settings you chose.

## Create your first habit

A habit is something you want to build, break or maintain. From the dashboard, give it a name and a schedule, then check in each day to start a streak. The full walkthrough is in [Create a habit](./habits/create.md).

## Tips

- **Start with one or two habits.** Consistency compounds faster than a long list you cannot keep.
- **Pick a real cue.** "After my morning coffee" beats "sometime today" — the [cue → routine → reward](./habits/create.md) loop is what makes a habit stick.
- **Check in honestly.** The [rope](./habits/rope-strength.md) is only useful if it reflects what actually happened.
