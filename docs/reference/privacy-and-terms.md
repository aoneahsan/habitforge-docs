# Privacy and terms

**HabitForge's authoritative privacy policy and terms live in the app itself.** This page summarises the essentials and links to the current, in-app versions.

## The official documents

- **Privacy policy:** [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/privacy](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/privacy)
- **Terms of service:** [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/terms](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/terms)
- **Delete your account:** [habitforge.aoneahsan.com/delete-account](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/delete-account)

The in-app pages are the source of truth. If anything here differs from them, follow the in-app versions.

## The essentials

- **Sign-in is Google-only.** HabitForge uses your Google account to identify you; there is no password to store.
- **Your data is your habits.** Habits, check-ins, streaks, achievements, and community activity are stored so the app can show them back to you.
- **Notifications are optional.** Push is off until you turn it on.
- **The community is opt-in.** Nothing about you appears in the community unless you choose to participate, and you control your visibility.

## Export and deletion

- **Export** your data from Settings.
- **Delete your account** from Settings or the [delete-account page](https://habitforge.aoneahsan.com/delete-account). Deletion removes your habits, check-ins, community posts, and profile. It cannot be undone.
